YES Master — User Guide

Master your track in real time — and see exactly what it did. Everything runs on your computer: no upload, no waiting, no black box.

Quick start (three moves)

  1. Import a track (drag it in, or click Import audio — or click Try a demo track if you have nothing to hand). Analysis runs automatically — loudness, peaks, dynamics, waveform.
  2. Pick a Style and a Loudness (Low / Medium / High). Press play and flip between Original and Mastered — the playhead stays put.
  3. Click Create Master. You get a clean 44.1 kHz / 24-bit WAV with a −1 dBTP ceiling, written next to your source file. Your original is never touched, and nothing is ever overwritten.

The two views

Standard is the whole flow in one screen: style, loudness, Create Master. Advanced opens the full room: eight styles, a 7-band EQ, compressor modes, width and warmth, live metering, loop regions, and delivery profiles. Same engine in both — Standard is not a lesser master.

Listening tools

Original vs Mastered — switch anytime while playing; the playhead is preserved so you compare the same moment, not two different bars.

Volume Match — evens out the loudness difference while you compare, so the louder one doesn't automatically "win." It is audition-only: it never changes your export. Off by default.

Preview LUFS — while playing Mastered, estimates where the export loudness will land, so what you hear tracks what you'll ship.

Loop region (Advanced) — Shift+drag on the waveform to mark a section, toggle Loop to hear just the chorus while you tweak. With Loop on, playback stays inside the region — clicking outside it snaps back in.

What "Width" actually does

Width scales the side signal of your track (the part that differs between left and right). 1.00 leaves the stereo image exactly as mixed; below 1.00 narrows toward mono; above 1.00 widens. Styles carry their own width as part of their character — some widen (Spatial), some deliberately narrow a little for focus (Tape, Oomph, Warmth).

While the slider shows AUTO, the preset's width is in charge (shown as "Auto · 1.11"-style next to the slider). The moment you drag it, your value replaces the preset's — so 0.05 doesn't mean "a little wider," it means "almost mono." 0.00 is full mono, not Auto. Use the ↺ AUTO chip (or double-click the slider) to hand width back to the preset.

If a rough mix suddenly sounds hollow or "mono-ish" under a narrowing preset, that's usually out-of-phase content in the mix being exposed as the sides come down — the math is honest; the mix is fragile. Try Universal or Spatial, or set Width above 1.00 yourself.

Reading your receipt

Every master ships with measured numbers from the rendered file — not estimates:

NumberWhat it means
LUFSOverall loudness. Streaming services normalize around −14 LUFS; louder targets trade dynamics for punch. Your call.
True peak (dBTP)The real inter-sample peak. Kept at or below the ceiling your profile promises (Standard: −1 dBTP).
Dynamic range (LU)How much the track breathes between its quiet and loud moments. Lower = denser.

Warnings are advisory — you can overcook your own track on purpose and the app will still render it; it just tells you the truth about the result. Only technical impossibilities (like a delivery format mismatch) are marked critical.

Album Master

One coherent record: consistent loudness across tracks, one delivery format, and an honest per-track manifest. An optional arc shapes loudness across the running order. Override exempts a single track completely — it renders with its own settings and its own loudness target (still in the album's format), and the manifest says so.

Your files & your privacy

Masters are written as new files — never over your source, never over an earlier render. Exporting twice gives you two files.

Your session autosaves locally; Save Project writes a portable .ams.json snapshot (it references your audio from disk — audio is not embedded, so keep the source files around).

Nothing you play, analyze, or render leaves this machine. There is no telemetry.

When something breaks

Headphones died / device vanished? Playback pauses with a banner; pick an output device and resume from the same spot.

Anything else weird? Open Help → Save diagnostics report. It writes one text file (recent app logs + session summary, assembled locally — nothing is sent anywhere). Send it with your bug report and the developer can usually see exactly what happened.

Shortcuts
