## Question and fixed comparison

On the five remaining known development sources (Funk, Metal, Aphelion, Rich,
Baby), how often does the existing normalized single-rule candidate satisfy the
unchanged C1 target and character limits with the corrected f64 production chain?
Fresh corrected controls avoid comparing new candidates to old f32 filter output.

- Universal 75, Custom -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP, 48 kHz stereo float delivery.
- Bind requested settings, source hash, original source anchors and operating
  drive to their completed C1 reports. No fresh coefficient or normalization
  policy, preset values, thresholds, or target tolerance are selected here.
- Each source gets current processing and the existing single-rule candidate,
  both through the current production chain/SRC/finalizer. Normalized input and
  source facts are prepared once and reused. This is ten fresh whole outputs.
- First run Coat control/-9 and Coat single/-14 as two exact reproduction anchors
  against the completed f64 E controls. Reuse their files after equality is proved;
  never replace them. These are harness checks, not a new Coat policy experiment.
- Use the unchanged `drive_metrics.py` source anchors, section/paired-attack,
  tone/stereo methods and individual limits. Compare each candidate with its
  matched fresh corrected control. No composite score or retuned bound.
- Independently check every complete output with the existing SOXR16/64 and
  loudness checks, plus source/output hashes, rate/frame/channel identity and
  finite PCM. The qualified native bound must meet the requested -1 ceiling.

## Conditional search and measurement

A technically valid single candidate with no character failures and target error
within 0.2 LU needs no extra candidate in this experimental rule. Otherwise mark
the case as needing further development. Do not assume attenuation improves every
metric; the prior Imaginal result disproves that. No additional candidates are
launched automatically by this checkpoint. A subsequent bounded comparison must
bind its own source/candidates/output budget before running.

The current tone/stereo limits depend on a matched current-processing control.
Therefore this experimental qualification costs **control plus candidate**, not
one total render for a new settings combination. A cached matching control can
be reused; different nonlinear settings cannot reuse it without proof. Report
both costs. A future production rule that avoids that control needs independently
validated source/preset limits; do not silently assume that work is free.

Record source decode/normalization, chain, SRC, finalization, metrics and independent
checking separately. Reusing source facts is evidenced by source/report hashes;
it is not a newly measured source-analysis speedup. Record the complete offline
job wall/CPU/memory cost separately from audio evaluation. Do not multiply a
single render time by four and call it measured search cost. Do not treat these
offline costs as app import or settings-change latency; the integration record
supplies separate native response observations.

Build/copy the runner before its output-budget preflight. Retain a 25 GiB free
reserve plus the estimated complete WAV bytes. Preserve all old reports, goldens,
failed cases and audio. No automatic production policy or sonic preference is
adopted. This is development on existing material, not C2 holdout evidence or C3
production integration.
