The central unresolved engineering question is the level-independent comparison
reference. A copy-specific current control changes the allowable tone/stereo
deviation and can change the winning candidate even with consistent normalized
PCM. Reusing a hypothetical original-level reference is useful for diagnosis
but cannot be the general product rule. Establish and test a reproducible
reference or justified absolute limits before C2; do not ask the owner to invent
numerical thresholds or call every movement toward the requested target an
improvement.

Source/preset/control policy is not finished by this table. Each required
behavior needs the active plan's corresponding processing, output and interaction
evidence before C3. Intentional voicing choices remain owner decisions when a
concrete unresolved tradeoff is ready for review.
