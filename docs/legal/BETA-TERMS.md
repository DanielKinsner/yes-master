## What you are getting

YES Master is a desktop mastering app for Mac and Windows. During the public
beta it is free. There is no account, no license key and no payment.

The beta runs for eight weeks from the day it opens. The end date is shown on
the website. When the beta ends, new beta downloads stop and beta support ends.
**The build you installed keeps working.** Nothing is switched off remotely.

A paid version may follow. If it does, buying it is optional, and its price and
terms will be published before anyone is asked to pay.

## Your audio and your data

- Your audio is processed on your own computer. It is not uploaded to us or to
  anyone else.
- The app has no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting.
- The app contacts GitHub to check whether an update exists. An update is
  downloaded only when you choose to install it.
- A diagnostics report is written only when you ask for one
  (**Help → Save diagnostics report**). It stays on your computer unless you
  decide to send it.
- YES Master never changes your source file. Exports are new files. Keep your
  own backups of work that matters, as you would with any beta software.
