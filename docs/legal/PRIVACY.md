Short version: **your audio never leaves your computer.** The app processes
everything locally. We collect almost nothing, and we don't track you.

## What the desktop app does and doesn't collect

- **Your audio:** processed entirely on your device. It is **never uploaded** to
  us or anyone else. We never see your files.
- **No analytics or telemetry:** the app does not include usage tracking,
  analytics, or crash reporting.
- **License activation:** when you unlock a paid license, the app makes a
  one-time online request to our licensing provider to validate your license key.
  That request includes your license key and minimal information needed to
  validate it (such as an order/device identifier). It does not include your
  audio.

## Information handled through the website / purchase

- **Email (beta and newsletter):** if you sign up on our website, we store your
  email with our email provider solely to send you the download link and product
  updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.
- **Purchases:** payments and license issuance are handled by our merchant of
  record, **Lemon Squeezy**, who acts as the seller. They process your payment
  details and billing information under their own privacy policy; we receive
  order confirmation and license data, not your full payment details.

## Third parties we use

- **Lemon Squeezy** — payments, invoicing, and license keys (merchant of record).
- **[Email provider — e.g. Buttondown / MailerLite / Kit]** — newsletter and beta
  download delivery.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Data retention

We keep your email until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Order and
license records are retained as required for support and accounting.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete the personal data we hold about you (your
email and order records) by emailing hello@yesmaster.app. Depending on where you
live (e.g. EU/UK/California) you may have additional rights; we will honor
applicable requests.

## Children

YES Master is not directed to children under [13/16] and we do not knowingly
collect their data.
