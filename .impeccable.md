## Design Context

### Users
Musicians and producers finishing their own tracks, often on the go. They need to import one song, make a few clear mastering choices, compare Original and Mastered confidently, and export a finished file without learning mastering engineering.

### Brand Personality
Confident, calm, and professional. YES Master should feel trustworthy, fast, local, and focused—not flashy, toy-like, or technically intimidating.

### Aesthetic Direction
A premium dark mastering-studio interface with focused navy surfaces, precise blue accents, clean hierarchy, restrained glow, and purposeful audio visualization. The current native iPhone app is the visual and interaction source of truth for Android mobile parity. Mobile is a pocket-sized expression of the same control-room vocabulary, not a shrunk desktop console.

### Design Principles
- Keep the one-track mobile workflow simple: import, analyze, choose, compare, export.
- Preserve the iPhone app's hierarchy, visual vocabulary, and touch-first behavior across Android while respecting platform conventions.
- Make processing state unmistakable with one restrained analysis moment; preserve reduced-motion accessibility and avoid decorative motion elsewhere.
- Prefer honest status and plain-language recovery over hidden failure or technical error text.
- Maintain legible contrast, large touch targets, scroll resilience, and screen-reader semantics on small screens and large text.
